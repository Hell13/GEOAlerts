OEZZ / Saudi Arabia 22-Oct A person was killed and several more were wounded in an attack on OEDF/Dammam in Eastern Saudi Arabia on Oct 21 - the latest in a string of Houthi strikes on Saudi airports. The US Embassy has issued a new security alert (attached) warning that further strikes are likely along with a ban on government employees from using any of the country’s airports without special permission. The situation continues to escalate. For a full risk briefing on the OEJD/Jeddah FIR, see safeairspace.net.
OEZZ / Saudi Arabia 21-Oct We’ve had another member report on Saudi airspace: flights from Egypt entering at SIKNO may be rerouted south via EVANO DCT MUVOL. OEGS/Gassim can fill up quickly and may reject diversions, so don’t use it as your sole alternate (I use OEMA/Madinah). Flights over OERK/Riyadh VOR may be rerouted north or south. Flights from OEDF/Dammam over OERK are usually sent via Gassim VOR before rejoining their route, adding 15-30 mins of fuel. Flights over OEYN/Yanbu VOR may be rerouted up to 50 NM east or west. OEYN/Yanbu is reportedly on indefinite hold and is not available as an alternate, despite no Notam.
XMID / 19-Oct Kuwait has reopened to overflights, bringing back the central routing option between Europe and the Middle East via Turkey-Iraq-Kuwait-Bahrain-Qatar-UAE. Flights can now cross the OKAC/Kuwait FIR between the OBBB/Bahrain and ORBB/Baghdad FIRs at FL280 or above. This route won’t be available to everyone, as EASA and several European countries advise against or prohibit overflights of Iraq. But for those allowed to use Iraqi airspace, this central routing offers an alternative to going through Saudi airspace where recent Houthi attacks have increased risks to aviation.
ZZZZ / Worldwide 19-Oct GPS Interference Update, Oct 19: GPS interference remains active across the usual hotspots - the SE Med, Gulf/Red Sea, Russia, India-Pakistan border and Myanmar. There was also some new jamming near Sao Paulo, plus a brief but intense spike in South Korea. The US is also seeing ongoing GPS interference testing, potentially affecting up to 100 flights a day. Smaller hotspots near Bogota, Mexico City, Kuala Lumpur, Hanoi and Guangzhou (sometimes Hong Kong) remain active from time to time. Thanks to GPSwise for this update!
EDDP / Leipzig Germany 19-Oct Police are investigating a security breach which caused disruptions on Oct 17. A vehicle managed to drive through a security fence before exiting the airport again.
