About SafeAirspace

Join OPSGROUP

The Conflict Zone & Risk Database provides a single, independent, and eternally free resource for all airspace risk warnings, so that pilots, dispatchers, and aircraft operators can easily see the current risk picture for unfamiliar airspace.

Safe Airspace is an initiative from OPSGROUP, an independent membership organization with 10,000 members from airlines, corporate flight departments, private operators, charter operators, military, and government.
